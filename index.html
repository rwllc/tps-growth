The Entrepreneurial Operating System gives every person in the organization a clear lens on what matters this quarter, what's broken, and what they own. Here's how each of the six components maps specifically to TPS.

Vision

Lock TPS's core values, 10-year revenue target, three-year picture, and one-year plan. Everyone navigates from the same map — no more priority conflicts between what you're chasing this week and what matters this year.

  • VTO session: core values + market focus
  • 10-year target: $30M locked
  • 3-year picture: new markets, new services
  • 1-year plan: $5.5M revenue, 3 federal wins
People

Right people, right seats — before you need them. The growth from 30 to 260 employees is a people problem more than a revenue problem. Solve it with predictive science, not gut feel.

  • Instinctive Drives + Axiology for key roles
  • Accountability Chart (not an org chart)
  • Continuity Files: every key person documented
  • Hiring predictability before headcount doubles
Data

Weekly Scorecard with 10 numbers that tell you at a glance if the business is on track — no narrative needed. If the numbers are green, good. If red, it goes on the Issues List. That's the whole meeting.

  • Estimate turnaround + win rate
  • Route efficiency + AR aging
  • Federal bid pipeline + active contracts
  • AI-generated brief delivered every Monday
Issues

Level 10 meetings every week. Issues List. IDS discipline: Identify, Discuss, Solve. No issue lives more than two weeks on the list. The ones that do are organizational problems, not operational ones.

  • Weekly L10 — 90-minute structured agenda
  • Issues List visible to leadership team
  • IDS: every issue resolved or escalated
  • AI-captured: decisions logged automatically
Process

Document the core processes that drive revenue. Estimating. Routing. Federal bidding. Onboarding. Handed off to the next person who needs them. The business shouldn't depend on who remembers how to do it.

  • Estimating SOP: 48 hrs → 24 hrs target
  • Federal bid SOP: SAM.gov to submission
  • Routing SOP: 300+ properties optimized
  • Onboarding SOP: scalable to 260 employees
Traction

Three Rocks per quarter. Assigned owners. Reviewed every week. No Rock lives in the maybe zone. This is how $4M becomes $30M — 90 days at a time, without losing the thread between quarters.

  • Q4 Rock #1: Win Bellevue municipal bid
  • Q4 Rock #2: Estimating SOP live + running
  • Q4 Rock #3: Scorecard 8+ consecutive weeks
  • Quarterly review: score, reset, go again
AI Quick Wins

What AI Changes for TPS — Specifically

These aren't hypothetical future applications. Each one is deployable within the first 90 days, and each has a measurable impact on the numbers that matter to a service business at this stage.

1

AI-Assisted Estimating — The Biggest Lever in the Business

Here's the situation: one estimator, two hours per estimate, a backlog that means clients sometimes wait a week to hear back. In a competitive bid environment, that's not a small problem — that's a close-rate problem. Most of the work doesn't get lost on price. It gets lost on silence.

The industry data is consistent: contractors who return estimates within 24 hours win at 60% or better. Under 24 hours could be a guarantee-able differentiator — a standard commitment nobody else in the market is making. With AI processing the site data, materials, and proposal formatting, the same estimator can get the work out the door the same day.

The goal isn't a modest improvement. If the current win rate is in the 20–30% range and 60%+ is achievable with 24-hour turnarounds, that's not a 7-point lift — it may be more than doubling the close rate on the same bid volume. That's the number worth building the whole estimating workflow around.

The core opportunity: 24-hour estimate turnaround, every time — potentially a guarantee. Win rate impact to be calculated from your actual bid data in month one.
2

Route Optimization for 300+ Properties

Static routes built by habit are costing you fuel, labor, and equipment hours every single day. AI-optimized routing for 300+ properties, adjusted seasonally for winter vs. standard service, reduces drive time by an estimated 10–15%. At your crew count and regional fuel costs, that's real margin recovered without adding a dollar of revenue.

Impact: 10% routing efficiency improvement on your actual labor + fuel costs across 300+ properties — measurable within 60 days
3

Federal Bid Monitoring — SAM.gov + WA Portals

Right now, federal bid opportunities sit in portals you check when you remember to. AI-monitored feeds for SAM.gov and Washington state procurement portals mean you never miss a contract that fits your certifications. First notice, every time, automatically — with an alert when something in your wheelhouse drops.

Impact: Additional federal contracts × your average contract value × 63% gross margin — the highest-margin lever in the business
4

Aspire CRM Automation

Follow-up emails, proposal reminders, renewal alerts, pipeline health checks — automated inside Aspire. The revenue that walks away because someone forgot to follow up on a $40K quote is the most expensive revenue you're not tracking. CRM discipline isn't a people problem when AI handles the cadence.

Estimated impact: 15–20% reduction in proposal ghosting; improved renewal retention rate
5

AI-Enhanced Federal Proposals

Federal proposals have requirements commercial bids don't. Compliance language, pricing justification, evaluation criteria formatting — AI assistance reduces prep time and raises submission quality. On federal evaluations, quality scores matter. A 5-point improvement in your scoring average is worth more than it sounds at $500K+ contracts.

Impact: Higher evaluation scores, faster prep, more bids submitted per procurement cycle
6

Meeting Intelligence (The Platform)

Every team meeting captured, speaker-identified, distilled into decisions, action items, and open questions. The strategy of one meeting stops bleeding into the chaos of the next. Institutional knowledge stops walking out the door when good people leave — because it's already in the system.

Impact: Faster decisions, cleaner accountability, zero lost context — measurable within 60 days
